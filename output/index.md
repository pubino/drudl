#  Inside ORFE 

## Writing Guidance

In writing, the department is identified as, 'Operations Research & Financial Engineering (ORFE)'.  Use the acronym 'ORFE' alone only if the full name and acronym have appeared at least once together in the same context.  Drop the parenthetical acronym from the full name if the acronym is already used elsewhere in the same context, avoiding unnecessary repetition.

## Graphical Identity

2025-Present ORFE Logo incorporating brand and graphic identity guidance from the [Office of Communications](https://communications.princeton.edu/guides-tools/logo-graphic-identity).

ORFE is visually distinct and recognizable by its logo, created in 1999 by [David Berstein](https://www.jmu.edu/cise/people/faculty/bernstein-david.shtml) while employed as at Postdoc at Princeton.

Logo Description

“The ORFE logo is a stylized matrix. The blocks of the matrix convey a sense of analysis, breaking down systems into their components. The matrix as a whole stands for synthesis, using ‘building blocks’ to construct large systems.”—Erhan Çinlar, Professor Emeritus & [Founding Chair and Faculty Member](https://orfe.princeton.edu/news/2019/twenty-years-later)

The ORFE logo's presentation aligns with [Princeton University's graphic identity](https://communications.princeton.edu/guides-tools/logo-graphic-identity) by utilizing **Medium grey** for the squares and optional full department name and **Princeton primary** for acronym lettering.

As part of the School of Engineering and Applied Science, ORFE representation should make a best effort to also incorporate [Princeton Engineering visual identity](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ts9ToV1awvufCZ4i7wDpCF5KFyftR2fY).

### Graphics for Suppliers

[Trademark & Licensing](https://trademarks.princeton.edu/) reviews all contracted vendor use of Princeton University logos, brands, and sub-brands, such as the ORFE logo, to ensure compliance when representing Princeton, the department, or both on ordered merchandise or other supplied materials.

If a vendor requires more than just the ORFE logo image, send the [ORFE Graphics Package for Designers](/document/121) zip file.  The files contained within the zip file are in Adobe Illustrator (.ai) format and will allow the vendor to create an accurate, scaled mockup sample and final product.

Per Section 3 of the [Princeton University Brand Guidelines](https://communications.princeton.edu/guides-tools/logo-graphic-identity), Princeton Orange, which ORFE makes best effort to use throughout, is #F58025, RGB 245, 128, 37, CMYK 0, 61, 98, 0 and Pantone Matching System 158 C / 158 U.

### Variants & Remixes

Variations of the logo are often required for application to material goods (e.g., swag).  The most frequently used and accepted variant is one without the full department text.

Variants may be used that employ neutrals dependent upon [accessible background contrast](https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/) and with care taken to [maintain accessibility](https://accessbility.princeton.edu) at small presentation sizes.

Remixes are playful alterations of the core elements of the ORFE logo, namely, the matrix and ORFE acronym, and are used sparingly for special events or for celebration or recognition on infrequent occasions.
