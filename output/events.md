The ORFE EventBrite team account is preconfigured for tax-exemption and payout.

Instructions for creating or reusing a Google Form to collect RSVPs for events, including setup, questions, and pre-filled links

How to automatically send a standard seminar announcement and guidance on how to schedule delivery to mailing lists.

[Coordinating Speaker Meetings](/events/coordinating-speaker-meetings)

The methods preferred by seminar organizers for coordinating meetings between faculty, students, and seminar guest speakers for lunch, dinner, and academic exchange.

[Create a New Calendar](/events/create-calendar)

A special method for creating new Google Calendars that preserves data backup and synchronization with other campus systems.

[Create a New or Rename an Existing Seminar Series](/events/create-new-seminar-series)

Create an entirely new seminar or series that will synchronize across calendars, appear on web sites and mailing lists, and that’s ready to coordinate with faculty. 

[Email Delivery and Access Rules](/news-communications/email-routing-access-delivery)

A map of email rules and permissions that describe what ORFE email mailboxes are shared and for what purpose.

[Invitations for Holiday Guests and Other Occasions](/events/invite-holiday-guests)

Management of invitations and responses for the annual ORFE Holiday Party.

[ORFE Mailing Lists](/news-communications/manage-list-permissions)

A map of permissions of ORFE affiliated mailing lists as well as how to add, remove, and otherwise manage subscribers.

[Prefilled Guest Reimbursement Form](/research-finance/custom-guest-reimbursement-form)

Generate a link that you can send to a guest that pre-fills select fields of the reimbursement form.

Requests are received as PDF email attachments sorted into the Guest Reimbursement Requests folder inside the Inbox of the **orfe-reimburse** Service Account.

[Prefilled Media Release Form](/events/prefilled-media-release-form)

Generate a link that you can send to a guest that pre-fills select fields of [the Media Release form](https://orfe.princeton.edu/media-release).

Completed releases are received as PDF email attachments sorted into the Completed Media Releases folder inside the Inbox of the **orfe-media** …

[Prepare the Podium](/events/prepare-podium)

Prepare the [101 - Sherrerd Hall](https://classroominfo.princeton.edu/View.aspx?bl_id=0653&fl_id=01&rm_id=0653_01_J15&bc=SHERH&img=0653101.JPG&rn=101) podium for seminar or other event speakers including how to have them [upload slides](/events/speaker-slide-collection) for display.

[Publish Events to Engineering Newsletter](/events/publish-events-engineering-newsletter)

A description of the automated process behind publication of ORFE Events to the Engineering Newsletter and how that process is monitored and managed.

[Slide Submissions Notifications](/events/slide-submissions-notifications)

Subscribe to the **orfe-notify-slides** list to receive an email notification when a speaker or anyone else [uploads slides](https://orfe.princeton.edu/slide-upload) via the link at the bottom of the ORFE Web Site.

Uploading slides is in keeping with the instructions on…

[Speaker Slide Collection](/events/speaker-slide-collection)

How to direct visiting speakers and guests to upload slides to the podium and how to retrieve them prior to their talk.

[Streaming, Recording, or Arranging Remote Speakers, Hosts & Talks](/events/stream-or-record-talk)

Requirements for streaming an event live, recording a talk, or both, including how to move a talk entirely online.

[Take Attendance with Event Check-in](/events/check-in)

Take event attendance by creating an Event Check-In Form link and displaying that link as a QR code.

[Tracking Visiting Speaker Tasks](/events/tracking-speaker-tasks)

The [Master Seminar List](/master-seminar-list) is a semi-automated spreadsheet that can be used to track tasks for visiting speakers like sending initial emails, collecting speaker information, and adding the speaker's talk to the ORFE Web Site.

[Update Channel Content on Public Displays](/events/update-channel-content-public-displays)

A description of the public display infrastructure and a guide for how to post to the channels available across all ORFE public displays.

[Use ORFE Meeting Spaces](/people-facilities/use-orfe-meeting-spaces)

An overview of the capabilities of various ORFE spaces in Sherrerd Hall and how to use them for meetings and events.

[Web Sites & Social Media, Public Displays & Signs](/essential-capabilities/web-sites-social-media-public-displays-signs)

An overview of ORFE's public-facing digital material such as the ORFE Web Site, social media pages, and digital displays.
